# Audio Assets

## 100hz.wav

The 100 Hz therapy tone the app plays. Generated by `scripts/generate_100hz.py`
— re-run that script to regenerate it; do not hand-edit the WAV.

**Specifications:**
- Pure 100 Hz sine, mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit
- Exactly 60.000 seconds (`useAudioSession` maps playback position 1:1 to the countdown)
- Raised-cosine fade in (0.5 s) / fade out (0.8 s) to avoid clicks
- Normalized to −1 dBFS peak (−4 dBFS RMS)

`100hz_origin.wav` is the previous asset, kept for reference. It is identical
apart from level (−6 dBFS peak / −9 dBFS RMS). Nothing loads it at runtime.

## Why 100 Hz — and what the evidence actually says

Full numbers, sample sizes, refuted claims and open questions:
**`docs/audio-therapy-evidence.md`**. Summary below.

One study supports it: Kagawa/Ohgami/Kato et al., Nagoya University,
*Environmental Health and Preventive Medicine* 2025 (PMC11955832). A 1-minute
100 Hz tone at 80–85 dBZ (≈ 61–66 dBA) taken **before** motion improved
posturography and lowered self-reported motion sickness.

Important limits — user-facing copy must not overstate this:

- **Unreplicated.** Single lab, self-controlled, unblinded, n≈82 across three
  provocations. Two authors work for DENSO; the stimulus is trademarked.
- **Delivery differs.** The study used bilateral loudspeakers 10–30 cm from the
  head, which may couple whole-head/bone vibration. Headphone equivalence is
  untested by anyone.
- **Timing differs.** The tone was prophylactic (before motion). Continuous
  listening during motion is listed by the authors as future work — the app
  therefore tells users to listen *before* setting off.
- **Level.** 61–66 dBA is normal conversation volume. Louder is not better, and
  frank air-conducted otolith activation (VEMP) would need 113–130 dB SPL —
  unreachable and unsafe on consumer headphones.
- The often-cited "the vestibular system is tuned to 100 Hz" result (Todd 2008)
  is a **bone-conduction** finding. Air-conducted otolith tuning peaks near
  500 Hz — but there is **no** human evidence that 500 Hz reduces motion
  sickness, so do not "fix" this by changing the frequency.

Do not claim the tone cures, treats, or prevents motion sickness.
